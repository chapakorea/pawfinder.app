PawFinder

Delete your account & data

This page explains how to permanently delete your PawFinder account, in line with Google Play's account and data deletion requirements. PawFinder is developed by chapakorea.

1In the app (fastest)

Delete from your device

If you still have PawFinder installed, this takes under a minute.

  1. 1

    Open PawFinder and make sure you're signed in.

  2. 2

    Go to the Settings tab.

  3. 3

    Tap the ⋮ menu next to "Settings", in the top right.

  4. 4

    Tap Account.

  5. 5

    Tap Delete account.

  6. 6

    Type your account email exactly as shown, to confirm it's really you.

  7. 7

    Tap Delete account again. Your account is deleted immediately — this can't be undone.

— OR —

2Without the app

Request deletion by email

No longer have PawFinder installed? Email us from the address you signed up with.

Send a message to contact@pawfinder.io ↗ with the subject "Delete my account" and include the email address registered to your account. We verify the request against that address and delete your account within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes the following, permanently:

Your data

  • Account & login credentials
  • Profile (display name)
  • Your lost/found pet reports & photos

Activity & connections

  • Your chat messages and conversations
  • Alert history and saved notification radius
  • Users you've blocked, and block records against you
Retention: deletion is immediate in our live systems. Encrypted backups are retained for up to 30 days as standard disaster-recovery practice, after which all remaining copies are purged.
